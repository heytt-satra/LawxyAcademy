Context Memory & Sub-Word Tokenization

Commercial contracts, court dockets, and patent claims contain dense, Latinate legal terminology. When processed by language models, these specialized terms decompose into numerous sub-word tokens, causing context windows to fill rapidly.

Core Rule: Never rely on monolithic zero-shot prompts for agreements exceeding 30 pages. Always apply structured chunking or RAG grounding to prevent attention loss.
